# RHM Church App Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 17 July 2026**

RHM Church operates the RHM Church App (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information the App handles

- **Content you create locally.** Notes, Bible highlights, search history, and radio recordings are stored on your device unless you choose to share them. They are not uploaded by the App for ordinary use.
- **Device and app activity information.** The App generates a random device identifier and may send limited activity information, such as app opens and active-session timestamps, to RHM Church's service providers to operate and improve the App.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the App obtains a push-notification token so RHM Church can send notices you have agreed to receive. You can disable notifications in your device settings.
- **Ads.** The App uses Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, and improve advertising. Where required, the App presents Google's consent form before requesting ads.
- **Media and communications.** If an authorised administrator uploads news, music, or other media through the App, the selected files and associated metadata are sent to RHM Church's storage and backend providers to publish them in the App.

## How information is used

We use information to provide App features, deliver radio and media content, send opted-in notifications, maintain security, understand aggregated App activity, and display and measure ads.

## Service providers

The App uses service providers such as Google (including AdMob and Google Play services), Expo, Supabase, and RHM Church's backend hosting provider. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms.

## Your choices

- You can manage notification permission in your device settings.
- You can delete locally saved notes, highlights, and recordings from the App or by uninstalling it.
- You can use **About → Ad Privacy Choices** to review or change advertising choices when that option is required for your region.
- You can reset or limit advertising identifiers in your device's privacy settings.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their country. If RHM Church learns that personal information was collected from a child without required consent, it will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## Data retention and security

Local content remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Server-side operational records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, security, and legal obligations. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The current version will be available at the privacy-policy link in the App and on the Google Play listing.
